# Unequal_Housing_Project_in_Boston
In April 2022, Health Affairs journal [published research](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UNsZFoiROnXif1XU6L2_kiELQ-xJmMia/view?usp=share_link) correlating racial makeup of neighborhoods to unequal housing conditions
and code enforcement related to asthma triggers. They used the publicly available dataset on Analyze Boston, However, a lot of internal data were left out of this study— so the Analytics team at Boston City Hall 
re-run the test to determine if factoring these data in change the findings.
A policy was implement for the City of Boston's Inspectional Services Department (ISD) to randomly inspect housing units in the city against housing code enforcement. The data collected from this inspection is
called the proactive dataset since the city took a proactive approach. We used this internal dataset to gain insights on the effects of molds, rodents/pests, or lack of heating/ventilation on Asthma disparities in Boston.
Also, we leverage census tract (Boston) data in our analysis.
